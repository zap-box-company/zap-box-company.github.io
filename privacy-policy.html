Privacy Policy

AI Cleaner - Phone Cleaner · Effective date: 8 October 2026

Summary. AI Cleaner scans your photos, videos, contacts and calendar on your device to find junk and duplicates. We do not upload, store or sell that content. We don't require an account. The current version is free, shows no ads, has no purchases and does not use an advertising ID. The only data that leaves your phone is anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can switch off in the App. Sections 3.1 to 3.5 describe features that are not part of the current version; they are listed so you know in advance how they will work if they are added.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Chircea Sebastian ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app AI Cleaner - Phone Cleaner (the "App"). We are the data controller for the limited data described here.

2. Data processed only on your device

To provide its core features, the App asks for access to data on your phone. This data is read and processed locally on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and we cannot see it.

Data / permissionWhy the App needs it
Photos and videos
(media / storage permission)		 Find similar and duplicate photos, screenshots, blurry photos, memes saved by chat apps, screen recordings, short clips and large videos; delete the items you select or move them to your phone's trash; move the items you choose into Secret Space. To do this the App measures each photo on your device (how sharp it is, and a short code used to compare it with other photos) and reads the text on images from chat apps and downloads. These measurements never leave your phone.
Contacts Find duplicate and incomplete contacts, merge or delete the ones you select, and create a contacts backup file that stays on your device.
Calendar and reminders Find old events and reminders and delete the ones you select.
Biometrics (Face / Fingerprint) Unlock Secret Space. Biometric checks are performed by Android. The App only receives a yes/no result and never has access to your biometric data.
Notifications
(only if you allow them)		 Tell you when a scan has finished, if you ask for that or have left the App while it runs; show one "time to clean" reminder a week if you turn it on; and, if you turn that on too, tell you when many new screenshots or photos have appeared (the App then counts new items in the background about once a day). All of these are created on your phone; no push service is involved.

Secret Space: your PIN is stored in Android's encrypted secure storage on your device. Photos and videos you hide are moved into the App's private storage on your device, which other apps and your gallery cannot read, and are removed from your gallery. They are not uploaded and are not included in device backups. We cannot recover a forgotten PIN, and uninstalling the App deletes the items still inside Secret Space.

Kept on your device only: contact backups, your cleaning history, your "Keep forever" list and the App's settings. The App is excluded from Android's cloud backup, so none of this is copied to your Google account. "Erase app data" in the App deletes all of it at once.

You can grant or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings. Features that depend on a permission will stop working without it; the rest of the App keeps working.

3. Data shared with service providers

3.1 Advertising – Google AdMob (not in the current version)

The current version shows no ads and does not read your advertising ID. If a later version adds ads, this policy and the App's store listing will be updated first, and this is how they will work: the free version would show banner and full-screen ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process: the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language), approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data (views and clicks). This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you have consented, personalise ads.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in U.S. states that require it, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change your choice later from the App.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • PRO users see no ads.
  • Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.2 Purchases – Google Play Billing and RevenueCat (not in the current version)

The current version has no purchases and does not contact RevenueCat. If paid plans are added: subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. To check whether you have PRO access, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the App, your purchase history for this App (products, dates, prices, transaction IDs), and basic device and app information. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email from us. RevenueCat Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy.

3.3 Email Breach Checker (only when you run a check)

This feature is not available yet; until it is, nothing is sent. Once it launches: when you enter an email address and start a check, that address is sent over an encrypted connection to Have I Been Pwned (haveibeenpwned.com) to look it up in databases of publicly known data breaches. The result is shown in the App. We do not store the email address or the result on our servers, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

3.4 Email Cleaner (not in the current version)

This feature is not available yet; until it is, the App has no access to any email account. Once it launches: if you connect a Google account, you sign in directly with Google (OAuth); we never see your password. With your permission, the App accesses your mailbox from your device to list senders, newsletters and junk messages, unsubscribe from senders you choose and delete or move to trash the messages you select. Email content and metadata are processed on your device and are not stored on or sent to our servers. You can disconnect at any time in the App or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI Cleaner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the feature or to comply with law, and do not allow humans to read it unless you give explicit consent for a specific message (for example, to investigate a support request), it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.

3.5 Invite friends program (not in the current version)

This program is switched off in the current version and nothing is sent. If it is switched on: when you open the invite screen, or install the App through a friend's invite link, the App sends our referral server (hosted on Cloudflare) your anonymous RevenueCat app user ID, your invite code or the code you were invited with, and your device's Android ID. We store the Android ID only as a salted one-way hash, used solely to prevent the same device from claiming rewards more than once. We use this data only to run the program and grant rewards through RevenueCat. We keep it while the program runs and delete it within 12 months after it ends, or sooner on request. Google Play tells the App which invite link an install came from (Install Referrer); no personal information about your friends is collected.

3.6 Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message (and the app version and device type that the App pre-fills in the subject line). We use this only to answer you and keep it for up to 24 months.

3.7 Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports – Aptabase

The App sends anonymous usage events to Aptabase (aptabase.com), hosted in the European Union: that the App was opened, the app version and build, the device's operating system version, locale and model type, whether an update notice was shown or accepted, and technical details of crashes. Aptabase does not use cookies, advertising IDs or any other persistent identifier, and we cannot link these events to you or your device. We use them only to count active installs per version, see how many users take an update and fix crashes. Events are kept in aggregate for up to 24 months. You can switch this off at any time in the App under About & Help → Preferences → Anonymous usage statistics; nothing is sent while it is off.

3.9 Google Play services used by the App

The version installed from Google Play uses Google Play's own components to check whether a newer version is available, to show Google Play's rating sheet, and to download the on-device text-recognition model used to find memes. These requests go from your phone to Google Play as they do for any app installed from Google Play; we do not receive any data from them. Your photos are never sent: text recognition runs on your device.

If you use a share button in the App, the App hands a short text or a picture it draws itself (for example "I freed 4.2 GB", never one of your photos) to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you choose to. The home-screen widget shows only how much storage is free.

3.8 Update checks (versions installed outside Google Play only)

Versions of the App downloaded directly from our website (not from Google Play) check a public file on GitHub (github.com) for new versions when opened and every few hours. GitHub receives your IP address as part of the request, as with any download. The version installed from Google Play does not do this; Google Play handles its updates.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Data processed on your device stays under your control. Uninstalling the App, or using "Erase app data" in the App, removes the App's local data, including the Secret Space PIN and anything still inside Secret Space. Items you delete for good cannot be recovered by us; items you move to the trash can be restored from your gallery's trash for 30 days.
  • Because the App has no accounts, we hold no profile about you. To ask for deletion of any data linked to you (for example support emails), email AppCleaner.Service@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

All network connections the App makes use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The Secret Space PIN is kept in Android's hardware-backed encrypted storage where available. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we design the App so that your personal content does not leave your device in the first place.
