Privacy Policy

AI Cleaner - Phone Cleaner · Effective date: 1 October 2026

Summary. AI Cleaner scans your photos, videos, contacts and calendar on your device to find junk and duplicates. We do not upload, store or sell that content. We don't require an account. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Payments are handled by Google Play; subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. Optional features (Email Cleaner, Email Breach Checker) only contact outside services when you choose to use them.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Chircea Sebastian ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app AI Cleaner - Phone Cleaner (the "App"). We are the data controller for the limited data described here.

Contact: AppCleaner.Service@gmail.com · Strada Mioriței 11, Nazna, Județul Mureș, Romania

2. Data processed only on your device

To provide its core features, the App asks for access to data on your phone. This data is read and processed locally on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and we cannot see it.

Data / permissionWhy the App needs it
Photos and videos
(media / storage permission)		 Find similar and duplicate photos, screenshots, blurry photos, screen recordings and short clips; compress photos and videos; delete the items you select.
Contacts Find duplicate and incomplete contacts, merge or delete the ones you select, and create a contacts backup file that stays on your device.
Calendar and reminders Find old events and reminders and delete the ones you select.
List of installed apps and app usage statistics
(only if you grant usage access)		 App Manager: show app sizes, sort apps by usage or size, and help you find apps you rarely use.
Biometrics (Face / Fingerprint) Unlock Secret Space. Biometric checks are performed by Android. The App only receives a yes/no result and never has access to your biometric data.

Secret Space: your PIN is stored in Android's encrypted secure storage on your device. Items you place in Secret Space stay on your device. We cannot recover a forgotten PIN.

You can grant or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings. Features that depend on a permission will stop working without it; the rest of the App keeps working.

3. Data shared with service providers

3.1 Advertising – Google AdMob (free version only)

The free version shows banner and full-screen ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and process: the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language), approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data (views and clicks). This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you have consented, personalise ads.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in U.S. states that require it, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change your choice later from the App.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • PRO users see no ads.
  • Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.2 Purchases – Google Play Billing and RevenueCat

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. To check whether you have PRO access, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the App, your purchase history for this App (products, dates, prices, transaction IDs), and basic device and app information. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email from us. RevenueCat Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy.

3.3 Email Breach Checker (only when you run a check)

This feature is not available yet; until it is, nothing is sent. Once it launches: when you enter an email address and start a check, that address is sent over an encrypted connection to Have I Been Pwned (haveibeenpwned.com) to look it up in databases of publicly known data breaches. The result is shown in the App. We do not store the email address or the result on our servers, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

3.4 Email Cleaner (only if you connect an email account)

If you connect a Google account, you sign in directly with Google (OAuth); we never see your password. With your permission, the App accesses your mailbox from your device to list senders, newsletters and junk messages, unsubscribe from senders you choose and delete or move to trash the messages you select. Email content and metadata are processed on your device and are not stored on or sent to our servers. You can disconnect at any time in the App or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI Cleaner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the feature or to comply with law, and do not allow humans to read it unless you give explicit consent for a specific message (for example, to investigate a support request), it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.

3.5 Invite friends program (only if you use it)

When you open the invite screen, or install the App through a friend's invite link, the App sends our referral server (hosted on Cloudflare) your anonymous RevenueCat app user ID, your invite code or the code you were invited with, and your device's Android ID. We store the Android ID only as a salted one-way hash, used solely to prevent the same device from claiming rewards more than once. We use this data only to run the program and grant rewards through RevenueCat. We keep it while the program runs and delete it within 12 months after it ends, or sooner on request. Google Play tells the App which invite link an install came from (Install Referrer); no personal information about your friends is collected.

3.6 Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message (and the app version and device type that the App pre-fills in the subject line). We use this only to answer you and keep it for up to 24 months.

3.7 Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports – Aptabase

The App sends anonymous usage events to Aptabase (aptabase.com), hosted in the European Union: that the App was opened, the app version and build, the device's operating system version, locale and model type, whether an update notice was shown or accepted, and technical details of crashes. Aptabase does not use cookies, advertising IDs or any other persistent identifier, and we cannot link these events to you or your device. We use them only to count active installs per version, see how many users take an update and fix crashes. Events are kept in aggregate for up to 24 months.

3.8 Update checks (versions installed outside Google Play only)

Versions of the App downloaded directly from our website (not from Google Play) check a public file on GitHub (github.com) for new versions when opened and every few hours. GitHub receives your IP address as part of the request, as with any download. The version installed from Google Play does not do this; Google Play handles its updates.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, other than the ad processing by Google AdMob described in section 3.1 (and, where required, only with your consent).
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, contacts, calendar, reminders or emails to our servers.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not track you: the anonymous statistics in section 3.7 contain no identifiers and are never used for advertising.

5. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

  • Performance of our contract with you: on-device cleaning features, purchases and PRO access.
  • Consent: personalised ads, the Email Cleaner connection, and device permissions. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised ads that fund the free version, fraud prevention and answering support requests, and anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (section 3.7) to keep the App working.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Data processed on your device stays under your control. Uninstalling the App removes the App's local data, including the Secret Space PIN. Deleting items inside the App cannot be undone by us.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide PRO access and to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Because the App has no accounts, we hold no profile about you. To ask for deletion of any data linked to you (for example support emails or RevenueCat purchase records), email AppCleaner.Service@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

All network connections the App makes use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The Secret Space PIN is kept in Android's hardware-backed encrypted storage where available. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we design the App so that your personal content does not leave your device in the first place.
